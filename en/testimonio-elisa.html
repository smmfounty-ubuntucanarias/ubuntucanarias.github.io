Testimony

A love story between two cultures

By Elisa Pérez
Founding member and volunteer at Ubuntu Canarias

A story shared in Voices of the World

At Ubuntu Canarias we believe every person has a story that deserves to be heard.

With Voices of the World we want to open a space where people can share their experiences, cultures and dreams in their own words, helping to build a more open, diverse and intercultural society.

The stories we publish do not claim to represent every migrant's experience. Each one reflects a personal reality, with its own circumstances, challenges and hopes. It is precisely that diversity of voices that enriches this project.

Today we share the testimony of Elisa Pérez, a founding member and volunteer at Ubuntu Canarias. She tells a love story with a Turkish man that began as a language exchange and changed the way she looks at other cultures and religions.

What a story leaves behind

At Ubuntu Canarias we believe that meeting other cultures is not always easy and does not always have a perfect ending, but it almost always changes us. Elisa’s story is about curiosity, about differences that weigh heavily and about getting to know yourself through someone else.

Listening to these voices helps us look at diversity with more honesty and fewer prejudices.

Editorial note. This testimony has been published with the permission of its author, Elisa Pérez, a founding member and volunteer at Ubuntu Canarias. The content reflects only the personal experience and opinions of the person who signs it.

Your story deserves to be heard too.
