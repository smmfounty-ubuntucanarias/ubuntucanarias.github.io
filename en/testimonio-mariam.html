Testimony

First in class, twice over

By Mariam El Ouali
South-eastern Morocco

A story shared in Voices of the World

At Ubuntu Canarias we believe every person has a story that deserves to be heard.

With Voices of the World we want to open a space where people can share their experiences, cultures and dreams in their own words, helping to build a more open, diverse and intercultural society.

The stories we publish do not claim to represent every migrant's experience. Each one reflects a personal reality, with its own circumstances, challenges and hopes. It is precisely that diversity of voices that enriches this project.

Today we share the testimony of Mariam El Ouali, 31, a young Moroccan man from the province of Sidi Slimane, who tells us about his wish to work, help his parents and build a better future without putting his life at risk.

A perspective to keep building bridges

At Ubuntu Canarias we believe that listening to stories like Mariam's helps us understand that access to education for women is not the same everywhere, and that holding on to a dream for years, amid responsibilities and decisions that are not always freely chosen, is also a form of strength.

Her testimony is a reminder that life paths don't always follow a straight line, and that time, together with persistence, can open up paths that at one point seemed closed.

Listening to these voices invites us to look at migration and identity from a more human perspective, based on respect, mutual understanding and intercultural coexistence.

Editorial note. This testimony has been published with the authorisation of its author, Mariam El Ouali. The content reflects exclusively the personal experience and opinions of the person who signs it.
