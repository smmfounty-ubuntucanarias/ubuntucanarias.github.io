Organise your job search

Ubuntu Canarias · 3 min read

Looking for work is tiring, especially when you don’t know if what you are doing is working. This guide helps you get organised. Swipe the cards to see it step by step.

1/8

Organise your job search

A step-by-step guide to looking for work in an organised way, without stress.

Swipe
www.ubuntucanarias.org
2/8

Get your papers ready

Before you start, prepare a folder.

  • NIE, TIE or passportAnd your work permit, if you have one
  • Padrón and qualificationsIf they are from another country, check if they are recognised
  • CV in PDFUp to date and easy to read
  • Everything on your phone and on paperSo you have it when they ask
3/8

Build a routine

Looking for work is a job too.

  • Fixed days and timesFor example, three mornings a week
  • A list of companiesWhere you would like to work, near your home
  • Small goalsHow many applications you will send this week
4/8

Look in reliable places

There are many fake offers. Be careful.

  • Canary Islands Employment Service (SCE)Register as a job seeker
  • Well-known job sitesInfoJobs, Indeed and company websites
  • Groups you trustLike the Ubuntu Canarias job group
  • Never pay for a jobNo personal details, no money over WhatsApp
5/8

Write everything down

If you don’t write it down, you forget who you wrote to.

  • Company and job
  • Date you applied
  • Contact and how you appliedWebsite, email, WhatsApp or in person
  • How it is goingSent, interview or not selected
6/8

Talk to people

Many jobs come through someone who knows you.

  • Say what you are looking forTo neighbours, friends and your community
  • Go to courses and workshopsYou learn and meet other people
  • Ask, and help tooIf you hear of a job, share it
7/8

Check what works

Once a month, look at your notes.

  • How many replies have you had?
  • How many interviews?
  • Where do you get more answers?

If nobody replies, change one thing: your CV or where you look. Not everything at once.

8/8

Save it and share it

To have it at hand and help someone else who is also looking.

Share itwith someone who needs it
