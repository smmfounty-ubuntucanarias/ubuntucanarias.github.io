Directory of social services in Gran Canaria: guidance, support and accompaniment

Ubuntu Canarias · 7 min read · Published on 8 November 2026

In Gran Canaria there are associations, foundations and social organisations that offer guidance and support with migration, employment, housing, disability, health and family support. Knowing where to go saves you time and journeys.

We have gathered organisations in Las Palmas de Gran Canaria and the surrounding area, sorted by area. Services, requirements and access conditions vary from one organisation and programme to another. Some offer direct support; others run specialised projects or represent a group and will point you to who can help. Before you go, call or write: check that they can attend your situation, whether they have places and whether you need an appointment.

Ubuntu Canarias does not manage the support these organisations provide. The contact details are the ones they publish themselves. If any of them does not work, let us know.

First of all: your social services centre

If you need help with food, paying rent or electricity, or applying for a benefit, the first step is usually the social worker in your municipality. Ask for an appointment at the social services centre in your neighbourhood (the town hall will tell you which one). The procedure varies from one municipality to another. For some kinds of support you will be asked to be registered at the town hall: we explain it in our registration guide.

If you need an organisation to submit an immigration application for you

There is an Electronic Register of Immigration Collaborators. Registered organisations can help you submit applications online if you authorise them to represent you. Being registered does not mean they handle every procedure, attend everyone or offer full legal advice.

According to the list published by the Government of the Canary Islands, these collaborating organisations work in Gran Canaria:

  • Asociación Benéfica Canoa Solidaria
    canoasolidaria.org/es/Nuestros-programas
  • ACAMEI (Asociación Canaria de Mediadores Interculturales)
    acamei.org/atencion-integral
  • Fundación Cepaim Attends people referred by other organisations, with a referral form.
    cepaim.org
  • Fundación Cruz Blanca
    fundacioncruzblanca.org
  • Fundación ECCA Social
    eccasocial.org/contacto
  • Provivienda
    provivienda.org
  • Programa Daniela (Oblatas) (Tenerife and Gran Canaria)
    oblatas.com/project/programa-daniela-oblatas

Before giving your details, ask which specific procedure they can help you with and whether they can attend you. You can check their registration in the official search tool of the Ministry of Inclusion.

The deadline to apply for the extraordinary regularisation ended on 30 June 2026. If someone tells you it is still open, be wary.

If you are looking for work

Every week we publish job offers in Gran Canaria and free courses. If you would like to work at one of these organisations, see our guide How to find a job in the third sector.

Before going to an organisation

Ask whether the support you need is free, whether you need an appointment and which documents to bring. An office address is not always a place where they see people without an appointment. Do not pay intermediaries to get an official appointment and do not trust promises of guaranteed regularisation.
