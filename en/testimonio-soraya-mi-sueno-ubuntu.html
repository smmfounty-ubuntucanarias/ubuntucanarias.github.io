By Soraya Mohamedi Mohamed Al Founty
Founder and president of Ubuntu Canarias · Psychologist

Voices of the World: stories in the first person

At Ubuntu Canarias we know that behind every migration journey there is a person with their own outlook, their own voice and their own way of telling it.

That's why we created Voices of the World: a space where those who have lived that experience — or, as in today's case, walked closely alongside it — can share it in their own words, without intermediaries, to build bridges between cultures.

No single story speaks for all the others. Each testimony is unique, with its own context and lessons, and it is precisely that plurality of voices that gives this project its meaning.

Today we share a second testimony by Soraya Mohamedi Mohamed Al Founty, founder and president of Ubuntu Canarias. In “The two pairs of glasses that taught me how to see the world” she talked about growing up between two cultures; here she tells the family and personal story from which Ubuntu Canarias was born.

A story that gives a project its name

At Ubuntu Canarias we believe that knowing where we come from helps to understand what we do. This story explains why the association focuses on informing, guiding and accompanying people, and why we think rules and humanity do not have to be at odds.

If you want to know how we work, visit What we do.
