Ubuntu Canarias · Updated every week

A space for people in Gran Canaria to find each other: people offering their services, individuals or small businesses looking for someone, and things that are given away or swapped.

Want to post an ad? It is free. Fill in the form, we review your ad and, if it follows the rules, we publish it here for 30 days.

There are no ads in this section yet. Would you like to be one of the first? Post your ad.
Before you answer an ad: Ubuntu Canarias reviews the ads, but does not know the people who post them and does not take part in any agreement. Ubuntu Canarias is not responsible for the content of the ads or for any agreements, services, jobs or exchanges arising from them. Never pay in advance, do not hand over original documents, and meet in public places. If an ad looks suspicious, let us know.

Noticeboard rules

  • Ads are free and are published for 30 days.
  • Gran Canaria ads only.
  • We do not publish ads that ask for money in advance, offers with illegal or discriminatory conditions (based on origin, religion, sex, age…), promises of papers or paperwork, or room or housing rentals.
  • The contact shown is the one the person chose to publish. We do not share any other details.
  • We may decide not to publish an ad or remove it at any time. If you want to remove yours early, write to us.
  • If you see an ad that breaks the rules or contains your data without your permission, write to info@ubuntucanarias.org and we will remove it as soon as possible.
  • To protect contact details, this page does not appear in search engines. More information in our privacy policy.
