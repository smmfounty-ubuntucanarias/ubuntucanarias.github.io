Quick guide, 2026 Regularisation: I now have my final permit

Guide written by Federación Sur Acoge · Shared by Ubuntu Canarias

This guide was written by Federación Sur Acoge. Ubuntu Canarias shares it because it can be useful to many people. The content and authorship belong to Sur Acoge.

If you obtained your permit through the 2026 Regularisation, the next step is to keep it and move forward. Federación Sur Acoge has prepared a simple guide to help you know:

  • When your permit ends and when to start the next procedure.
  • What to do if you are working or have a job offer.
  • What options you have if you do not have a job yet.
  • Which documents are important to keep.

Start preparing in good time. Knowing your next step can help you avoid problems and exercise your rights.

Download the guide (PDF) See it on the Sur Acoge website

The guide is in Spanish. Full title: «Guía rápida Regularización 2026. Ya tengo mi autorización definitiva. ¿Qué tengo que hacer para mantenerla y seguir adelante?».
