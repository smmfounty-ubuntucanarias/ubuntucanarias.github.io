Intercultural skills and strategies to fight disinformation

El Patio de las Culturas · Canarias Convive programme · Instagram
  • DatesLast week of October or first week of November (to be confirmed)
  • ScheduleAfternoons, 17:00 to 20:00
  • VenueC/ Doctor Chil, 15 (Centro Loyola), Las Palmas de Gran Canaria
  • Length12 hours · in person
  • CertificateIssued by the organisation, backed by the Fundación Universitaria de La Laguna
  • PriceFree

Practical training to identify hate crimes and disinformation in the media and on social networks, work on intercultural skills and build community networks to resolve conflicts. Active participation is required. The course is taught in Spanish.

Programme
  1. Module 1 · Understanding diversity and interculturalityMonday, 17:00–20:00 (3 hours)Aim: Understand the key concepts of cultural diversity, interculturality, inclusion, racism and discrimination, and how they help to interpret real situations.
  2. Module 2 · Racism, discrimination, inequality and exclusionTuesday, 17:00–20:00 (3 hours)Aim: Analyse the social and historical construction of racism and white privilege, using an intersectional lens to understand how different forms of discrimination and inequality interact.
  3. Module 3 · Taking a stand and building responsesTime to be confirmedAim: Tell apart facts, beliefs, hoaxes, discriminatory speech and hate speech or hate crimes, within specific social contexts.
  4. Module 4 · Community actionThursday, 17:00–20:00Aim: Design and apply practical strategies with an intercultural, sector-based, community and human rights approach, adapted to different fields of action.

  • Who forProfessionals who, through their work, are in contact with migrant women, teenage girls and girls, in formal and/or non-formal education

Specialised short course (2nd edition) of the LAB MF programme.
