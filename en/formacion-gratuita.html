Intercultural skills and strategies to fight disinformation

El Patio de las Culturas · Canarias Convive programme · Instagram
  • DatesLast week of October or first week of November (to be confirmed)
  • ScheduleAfternoons, 17:00 to 20:00
  • VenueC/ Doctor Chil, 15 (Centro Loyola), Las Palmas de Gran Canaria
  • Length12 hours · in person
  • CertificateIssued by the organisation, backed by the Fundación Universitaria de La Laguna
  • PriceFree

Practical training to identify hate crimes and disinformation in the media and on social networks, work on intercultural skills and build community networks to resolve conflicts. Active participation is required. The course is taught in Spanish.

Programme
  1. Understanding diversity and interculturality · Monday, 17:00–20:00
  2. Racism, discrimination, inequality and exclusion · Tuesday, 17:00–20:00
  3. Taking a stand and building responses: facts, hoaxes and hate speech · time to be confirmed

Ubuntu Canarias is a member of the COMPASS Collaboration Network – Observatory of Human Mobility between the Canary Islands and Africa (Interreg MAC 2021-2027 Programme)

We work alongside Casa África, public institutions and West African bodies for a comprehensive and human approach to migration flows.

Learn more about COMPASS

We support migrants through their social, employment and community integration process in the Canary Islands.
