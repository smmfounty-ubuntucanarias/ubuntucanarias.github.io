Ubuntu Canarias is a member of the COMPASS Collaboration Network – Observatory of Human Mobility between the Canary Islands and Africa (Interreg MAC 2021-2027 Programme)

We work alongside Casa África, public institutions and West African bodies for a comprehensive and human approach to migration flows.
