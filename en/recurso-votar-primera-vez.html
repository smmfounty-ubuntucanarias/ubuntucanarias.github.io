How to vote for the first time in Spain

Ubuntu Canarias · 5 min read · Published on 27 November 2026

With Spanish nationality you also have the right to take part in elections. This guide explains, step by step, how to check if you are on the electoral roll, where to vote, which document to bring and how voting works.

General election on Sunday 29 November 2026

  • Polling stations are open from 9:00 to 20:00.
  • The electoral roll closed on 1 August 2026 is used. If you got Spanish nationality after that date, you may not yet be on the roll for this election. Check with the INE or call the Electoral Roll Office: 900 343 232.
  • The deadline to request a postal vote has already passed.

1. Do I have the right to vote?

If you have Spanish nationality, you can vote if:

  • You are 18 or over on election day.
  • You are registered on the electoral roll.
  • You do not have a final conviction that takes away your right to vote.

You can vote in general, regional, municipal, island council (cabildo) and European elections, according to the requirements of each election.

2. Check that you are on the electoral roll

Having a Spanish ID card does not mean you are already on the roll for a specific election. To check:

  • Go to the INE online office, «Consulta de inscripción en el Censo Electoral».
  • Identify yourself with Cl@ve or an electronic ID card.
  • You will see whether you are registered and, when there is an election, your polling station and table.

Check the electoral roll at the INE (in Spanish)

3. Where do I vote?

When there is an election, you receive at home an electoral card (tarjeta censal) with your polling station, the address, the section and the table. You do not need to bring it to vote. You can also check this information at the INE.

4. Which document do I bring?

  • Spanish ID card (DNI), the physical card or on your phone with the MiDNI app.
  • Passport with a photo.
  • Driving licence with a photo.

Photocopies and the receipt of a DNI being processed are not accepted.

5. How do I vote?

  1. Go to your polling station and find your table.
  2. Take the ballot papers from the booths or from the tables set up for that.
  3. Put the ballot paper in its envelope.
  4. Give your document to the people at the table.
  5. Hand over the envelope or put it in the ballot box, as they tell you.

6. What if I work that day?

You have the right to paid leave to go and vote. In this election, up to 4 hours within your working hours.

7. What if I cannot go in person?

There is a postal vote, but you have to request it within the deadline for each election. If you request it, you can no longer vote in person in that election.

8. Your rights

  • Your vote is free and secret.
  • No one can force you to vote for a party or to say who you voted for.
  • You can cast a blank vote.
  • Voting is not compulsory in Spain.
