María Elena, a story I found at a bus station

Voices of the World · Testimony collected by the Ubuntu Canarias team

A space to listen to stories

At Ubuntu Canarias we believe that every story can inspire, bring cultures closer together, and help us build a more open, intercultural society.

That is why Voices of the World exists: a space to share stories, cultures, lessons and experiences that let us see the world through other people's eyes. Because sometimes an unexpected conversation teaches us far more than we imagined.

This is one of those stories.

Voices of the World

At Ubuntu Canarias we want Voices of the World to be exactly that: a space to listen. Because getting closer to other people doesn't just mean learning about their countries of origin, their customs or their cultures. It also means learning about their paths, their losses, their lessons, their hopes, their contradictions and their ways of holding on.

María Elena's story doesn't have a simple moral, and we don't know how it will continue either. Maybe the relative she's waiting for will arrive soon. Maybe she'll find a room. Maybe she'll accept help. Maybe not.

What we can do is remember that behind every person there is a story we don't see at first glance.

That afternoon I missed a bus. And, thanks to that, I found a conversation. I found María Elena. For a few minutes, two strangers shared something far more important than a station: a moment of humanity.

Because, in the end, that is also what Voices of the World is about: coming closer to one another to remember that, before any label, we are people.

Editorial note. This account is shared in a way that protects the protagonist's identity: her real name has not been used. The facts included are those she herself shared during a brief conversation, with no information added or verified beyond what she told us.

Your story deserves to be heard too.

Every experience brings a unique view of the world. In Voices of the World we want to keep building a space where people can share their stories, cultures and lessons to bring us closer together.
