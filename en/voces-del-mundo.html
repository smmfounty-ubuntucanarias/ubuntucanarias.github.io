First testimony

My name is Yassine and this is my dream

By Yassine E.
31 years old · Morocco

A story shared in Voices of the World

At Ubuntu Canarias we believe every person has a story that deserves to be heard.

With Voices of the World we want to open a space where people can share their experiences, cultures and dreams in their own words, helping to build a more open, diverse and intercultural society.

The stories we publish do not claim to represent every migrant's experience. Each one reflects a personal reality, with its own circumstances, challenges and hopes. It is precisely that diversity of voices that enriches this project.

Today we share the testimony of Yassine E., 31, a young Moroccan man who tells us about his wish to work, help his parents and build a better future without putting his life at risk.

A perspective to keep building bridges

At Ubuntu Canarias we believe that listening to stories like Yassine's helps us understand that behind every migration process there are people with responsibilities, abilities, hopes and life projects.

His testimony reflects a concrete reality and a personal decision: to pursue his goals through safe and legal means, convinced that no dream justifies putting one's own life at risk.

Listening to these voices invites us to look at migration from a more human perspective, based on respect, mutual understanding and intercultural coexistence.

Editorial note. This testimony has been published with its author's authorisation. To protect his privacy, some identifying details have been partially anonymised. The content reflects exclusively the personal experience and opinions of the person who signs it.
