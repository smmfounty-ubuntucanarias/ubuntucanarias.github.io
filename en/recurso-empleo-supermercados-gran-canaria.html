Looking for supermarket jobs in Gran Canaria?

Ubuntu Canarias · 3 min read

We have gathered some of the main supermarkets and retail chains in Gran Canaria. On their websites you can check job offers or send your application.

Ubuntu Canarias does not manage these offers or take part in the selection processes. We share the official links to make your search easier.

Supermarkets and chains

HiperDino / SuperDino

One of the main chains in the Canary Islands, with many stores in Gran Canaria. It usually posts offers for store staff, fresh food counters (butcher, fishmonger), shelf stocking, warehouse and office roles.

hiperdino.es/empleo

SPAR Gran Canaria

It has stores in several municipalities on the island and its own job portal, where you can register, upload your CV and see the current offers.

spargrancanaria.es/empleo

Mercadona

With supermarkets in Las Palmas de Gran Canaria, Telde, San Bartolomé de Tirajana and other municipalities. On its portal you can filter vacancies by location.

Mercadona job portal

Lidl

It has supermarkets and logistics facilities on the island. It posts roles such as cashier/shelf stacker, store manager, warehouse and others.

empleo.lidl.es

ALDI

With stores in Las Palmas de Gran Canaria and Telde, among other places. It offers store, management, logistics and other roles.

aldi.es/empleo

Carrefour

Hypermarkets and supermarkets in Las Arenas, La Ballena, Hoya de la Plata and Vecindario, among others. Applications for Carrefour Express and Carrefour Market also go through this portal, where you can search vacancies by province.

Work at Carrefour

DIA

It has stores in Gran Canaria. Its portal lets you search for cashier and stocking roles, store managers, fresh food, warehouse and other jobs.

empleo.dia.es

Canarian companies and local supermarkets

Grupo Bolaños

A Canarian company with supermarkets, Cash & Carry stores and other businesses in Gran Canaria. You can send your CV by following the instructions on its job page or by writing to personal@grupobolanos.com.

grupobolanos.com/gran-canaria/empleo

UNIDE

A supermarket network that is growing on the island. Some stores hire their own staff, so it is also worth asking directly in the shop.

tu.unidesupermercados.es

Hipercor – El Corte Inglés

Hipercor Siete Palmas, in Las Palmas de Gran Canaria. As well as food retail roles, the group's portal lists offers in other areas.

empleo.elcorteingles.es

Makro

A store in Telde, aimed mainly at hospitality and retail professionals.

makro.es/carrera

Covirán

It works as a network of member supermarkets, and hiring usually depends on each store. It is worth handing in your CV in person.

coviran.es

What roles are they looking for?

The sector has openings for cashiers/shelf stackers, butchers, fishmongers, fruit and vegetables, bakery, warehouse and order picking, cleaning and maintenance, store supervisors, administration, logistics and transport. Not every company is hiring for all these roles at the same time.

Before you apply

  • Have an up-to-date CV in PDF.
  • Make your experience and availability clear.
  • Check which municipality the job is in and how you would get there.
  • Read the requirements before applying.
  • If there is no suitable offer, check whether the company keeps a candidate pool or accepts speculative applications.

Never pay to get a job. And don't send personal documents by WhatsApp to people you don't know: always use each company's official channels.

Ubuntu Canarias shares resources and job offers to make it easier to access work opportunities. We are not an employment agency and we do not act as intermediaries in hiring.
