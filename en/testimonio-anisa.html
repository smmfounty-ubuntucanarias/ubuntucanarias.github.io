Testimony

Five trips to Turkey and a path I did not expect

By Anisa
Voices of the World

A story shared in Voices of the World

At Ubuntu Canarias we believe every person has a story that deserves to be heard.

With Voices of the World we want to open a space where people can share their experiences, cultures and dreams in their own words, helping to build a more open, diverse and intercultural society.

The stories we publish do not claim to represent every migrant's experience. Each one reflects a personal reality, with its own circumstances, challenges and hopes. It is precisely that diversity of voices that enriches this project.

Today we share the testimony of Anisa, the name she uses to sign this story. She tells how a dream she had kept for years, seeing Cappadocia, took her to Turkey five times, to hear the adhan for the first time and, in time, to find her path in Islam.

What her story leaves us

At Ubuntu Canarias we believe that getting to know other cultures up close, without intermediaries, changes the way we look at the world and also the way we look at ourselves. Anisa’s story is about a dream come true, curiosity for other languages and a personal path that is not always easy to share with the people we love most.

Her testimony reminds us that many of the ideas we have about other religions and cultures do not come from experience, but from what we have heard. Listening to these voices invites us to come closer with more respect, more questions and fewer prejudices.

Editorial note. This testimony has been published with the permission of its author, who signs with the name Anisa to protect her anonymity. The content reflects only the personal experience and opinions of the person who signs it.

Your story deserves to be heard too.

Every experience brings a unique view of the world. In Voices of the World we want to keep building a space where people can share their stories, cultures and lessons to bring us closer together.
