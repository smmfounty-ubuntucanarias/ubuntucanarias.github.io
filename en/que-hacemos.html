What we do

Full support, step by step

From first contact to full independence, we offer comprehensive support tailored to each person and each stage of their process, in person or remotely.

Our purpose

Reception, integration and the defence of rights

Ubuntu Canarias is a non-profit association working for the social, legal and employment reception and integration of migrant people and their families in the Canary Islands. We combine legal guidance on immigration, asylum and international protection with psychosocial support, intercultural mediation, employment guidance and community awareness-raising, with the aim of facilitating equal access to rights, resources and opportunities.

Who is our work aimed at?
  • Migrant people and their families
  • Unaccompanied minors
  • Migrant women in vulnerable situations
  • Victims of trafficking and gender-based violence
  • People seeking international protection and asylum
  • The host society, through community awareness-raising
Areas of work

Our main lines of work

In line with our statutes, we organise our activity around five main areas of work. Training is added as a complementary activity, present across all of them.

01

Legal guidance

Information and support on immigration, asylum and international protection, together with assistance in administrative procedures. We listen to each situation before pointing a way forward, so every person understands their real options.

We offer technical guidance on immigration, asylum, international protection and regularisation procedures. If you have questions about your legal situation or need advice, get in touch with us.

Ask your legal questions
02

Integration and initial reception

We support the settling-in process: facilitating access to housing, education, and social and health services, and connecting each person with local associations and neighbourhoods across the Canary Islands.

Request support with procedures Just arrived? Join our WhatsApp group for newcomers
03

Employment guidance and job placement

We design personalised employment pathways, carry out job prospecting and support active job-seeking, working with companies to facilitate the hiring of migrant people.

Get job listings
04

Training

As a complementary activity to our main areas, we run Spanish language courses, digital literacy and pre-employment training, together with workshops on transversal skills that open doors to new opportunities.

📌 Interested in a Spanish course or preparing to apply for nationality? We're currently collecting requests to organise upcoming groups based on demand.

05

Psychosocial support

We provide individual and group psychological support, with particular attention to migratory grief, emotional accompaniment and referral to specialised mental health resources when needed.

💬 Do you need emotional support, or would you like to join one of our mutual support groups on migratory grief? Fill in your details so we can get in touch and tell you about upcoming sessions.

06

Intercultural mediation and awareness-raising

We facilitate communication and conflict resolution between people of different cultures, institutions and the host society, and run talks, workshops and campaigns to combat racism and xenophobia.

🤝 Do you need mediation support with an organisation, service or neighbour, or would you like to request an awareness workshop or talk for your centre or group? Contact us and we'll look into your case.

More areas of work

Other areas that are also part of our work

Our statutes also cover the following lines of action, which we develop according to identified needs and available resources.

Volunteering

Recruitment, training and coordination of volunteers as a channel for community participation.

Groups in particularly vulnerable situations

Specific attention to unaccompanied minors, migrant women, victims of trafficking or gender-based violence, and people seeking international protection.

Initial reception

Basic information, guidance and referral to the relevant public services in the first moments after arrival.

Administrative procedures

Support with general procedures such as municipal registration, the health card, or booking appointments with public administrations.

Advocacy and network work

Participation in third-sector networks, platforms and forums, and collaboration with public administrations, universities and other organisations.

Materials and outreach

Production of guides, leaflets and digital content on immigration, international protection and social resources, in several languages.
