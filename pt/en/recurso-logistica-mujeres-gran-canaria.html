Logistics is for women too: where to look for work in Gran Canaria

Ubuntu Canarias · Talento Sin Fronteras · 4 min read · Published on 7 November 2026

Warehouses, transport and distribution employ a lot of people in Gran Canaria. We have put together companies, temp agencies and job sites where you can look, plus a few ideas to make your application stronger.

Driving a forklift, organising a warehouse or picking orders are jobs that women and men can do equally well. If you have a forklift course or are thinking of doing one, this guide is for you. And if you are a woman, even more so: there are still few women in this sector, and there is no reason for it to stay that way.

Ubuntu Canarias does not manage these offers or take part in recruitment. We share the official links to make your search easier.

What jobs can you look for?

Forklift driver, warehouse operative, logistics operative, order picker, goods reception, stock and inventory control, loading and unloading, and distribution staff.

To make your application stand out

  • Put your forklift course on your CV and say which types of forklift you can drive (counterbalance, reach truck, electric pallet truck).
  • Add your health and safety training.
  • If you can use a handheld scanner (PDA) or barcode reader, say so: it is often required.
  • Read each offer carefully: shifts, experience, driving licence and whether you need a vehicle to get to the industrial estate.

If you are a woman thinking about training

Do not rule out this job because it has traditionally been linked to men. There are women who drive forklifts, run warehouses and coordinate distribution. It is not about being given the job for being a woman, but about being able to apply for it on equal terms.

If you want to train, our free training page has forklift and logistics courses, some of them only for women.

Never pay to get a job. And do not send personal documents by WhatsApp to people you do not know: always use each company’s official channels.
